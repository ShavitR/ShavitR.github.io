How to sell digital downloads without Stripe

Stripe is the default answer for selling digital goods - and the wrong one for plenty of sellers: unsupported countries, account freezes, chargebacks, and fees that hurt $5 products. Here is the landscape as of 2026, and a fully self-hosted route.

Why sellers look for alternatives

  • Availability: Stripe is not open to merchants in many countries.
  • Account risk: a single dispute wave can freeze your balance for months.
  • Small-ticket economics: ~30¢ + 2.9% is brutal on a $3 preset pack.
  • Privacy: some buyers simply prefer paying crypto without creating accounts.

The self-hosted route: CryptoPaywall

CryptoPaywall is an open-source paywall you deploy to Vercel in minutes. The buyer flow:

  1. Buyer sends USDC from any wallet to your address directly - Ethereum, Base or Polygon all work.
  2. Buyer pastes the transaction hash into your storefront page.
  3. A serverless function verifies the transfer on-chain - correct token contract, correct recipient, sufficient amount - via public RPCs. No API keys.
  4. An HMAC-signed link unlocks the download automatically.

No database, no accounts, no middleman. Money never touches anyone else's server.

Honest trade-offs

  • Card payments: none. Your audience needs a wallet. For dev tools, crypto-adjacent products and privacy-minded audiences this is fine; for grandma-facing cookbooks it is not.
  • Refunds are manual: you send USDC back yourself - which also means no automated chargeback fraud against you.
  • Taxes: crypto received is still income where you live. Keep records; nothing here is tax advice.
  • Traffic is still your job: like every self-hosted option, nobody sends buyers to your door.
