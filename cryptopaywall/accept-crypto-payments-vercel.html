Accept crypto payments on Vercel in 5 minutes

USDC on Ethereum, Base or Polygon - verified on-chain by your own serverless function, with automatic delivery of your digital file. No Stripe, no Coinbase Commerce account, no API keys anywhere.

What you end up with

  • A storefront page at your-project.vercel.app showing your price and payment QR.
  • Buyers send USDC to your wallet and paste the transaction hash.
  • Your function checks the blockchain: did this exact transaction transfer enough USDC to you? If yes → signed download link.
  • Money lands in your wallet instantly. There is no step where a platform holds it.

The five minutes

  1. Get the code: clone ShavitR/cryptopaywall, or click its Deploy-with-Vercel button.
  2. Prepare the variables: from the repo root run 
    node bin/prepare.js your-file.zip --name "My Product" --price "$9 USDC"
    This base64-encodes your file and prints every environment variable, including a freshly generated signing secret and a default multi-chain config.
  3. Paste them into Vercel's environment variables screen (Project → Settings → Environment Variables).
  4. Add your wallet address as MERCHANT_ADDRESS. This is where payments land.
  5. Deploy. Share the URL.

How the verification actually works

When a buyer submits a hash, the function calls eth_getTransactionReceipt against public JSON-RPC endpoints for each configured chain. It walks the receipt's event logs looking for an ERC-20 Transfer event where:

  • the emitting contract is the token you accept (e.g. native USDC on that chain),
  • the to field equals your wallet,
  • the transferred amount clears your minimum,
  • and the transaction itself succeeded (status == 0x1).

If all four hold, it issues an HMAC-signed link valid for 24 hours. Nobody can forge one without your secret, and nobody can claim a payment that does not exist on-chain.

Costs

Vercel hobby tier covers this comfortably at low traffic. Public RPCs are free but rate-limited; swap in your own endpoints via config when you grow. Network fees are paid by buyers in gas - fractions of a cent on Base or Polygon.
